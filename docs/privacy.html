Lakeline for Databricks

Privacy policy

Your data stays between your browser and your Databricks workspace.

Effective 16 October 2026 · applies to the Lakeline for Databricks browser extension and its optional local helper.

Summary

  • Lakeline has no servers. The developer receives none of your data: no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports.
  • The extension sends requests only to the Databricks workspaces you connect, and, if you use it, to the optional local helper on your own computer (127.0.0.1).
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Websites, including Databricks pages, can't read anything the extension stores. Browser extension storage is only available to the extension itself.

What the extension sends, and to whom

  • Your Databricks workspace: read-only SQL queries against Databricks system tables (billing, query history, job and pipeline timelines), and read-only API calls for SQL warehouses, job runs, pipelines and clusters. If you ask Genie a question, the question text goes to the Genie Agent you selected in your own workspace. Requests carry your sign-in token, which goes only to that workspace.
  • Databricks sign-in: "Sign in with Databricks" uses your workspace's own OAuth endpoints, through the public OAuth client that Databricks provides for its own CLI (databricks-cli; Databricks did not create it for Lakeline and does not endorse Lakeline) or your organization's app connection. With the CLI's public client, the sign-in ends at http://localhost:8020: the extension reads the one-time code from the address of the sign-in tab it opened, then closes that tab. Nothing is sent to, or served from, your computer.
  • The optional local helper (if you choose to run it): it runs on your computer at 127.0.0.1, and its Databricks credentials stay in the Databricks CLI on your computer.

The extension does not read the text of your SQL queries or the result rows of your queries in Query History. Cost estimates use only timestamps, durations, warehouse IDs and billing records.

Permissions

Site access is requested one workspace at a time, only when you connect it. The extension never asks for access to all websites. To finish "Sign in with Databricks", it also asks once for localhost:8020, used only to read the address of its own sign-in tab. Desktop notifications are optional and contain no query data. See the Chrome permissions documentation for what each permission means.

Your choices

  • Disconnect a workspace or remove a token in Connection preferences.
  • Clear Genie conversations with "Clear now" in the side panel.
  • Remove the extension to delete everything it stored in your browser.
  • If you used the local helper, you can remove its Databricks CLI profiles (names start with dcc-) with the Databricks CLI.

Children

Lakeline is a tool for Databricks users at work and is not directed to children.
