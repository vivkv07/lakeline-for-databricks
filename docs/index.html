Lakeline for Databricks

Browser extension

Costs, runs and Genie answers, right where you work in Databricks.

Lakeline shows estimated spend inside your workspace, adds a cost badge to every job run, keeps an eye on your runs and compute, and lets you ask Genie Agents from a side panel while you keep working.

Add to Chrome

What you get

  • Cost banner: the last 7, 30 or 90 days, compared with the period before, with freshness shown.
  • Badges: cost per job, pipeline, warehouse, notebook and app, and per job run (including the pipelines a job triggered).
  • Query History: per-query estimates with the assumptions explained.
  • Cost Explorer: products, top drivers, runs, calendar, SKUs, daily detail, CSV export.
  • Run monitor: running and failed jobs, pipelines, warehouses and clusters, in a small window that can float on top.
  • Ask Genie: pick a Genie Agent, ask, keep working; answers come back with a table, a chart and the SQL.

Trustworthy by design

  • Read-only SQL on your own Databricks system tables; every amount is labeled as an estimate.
  • No analytics and no developer servers. Data goes only to your own workspace.
  • Access is requested one workspace at a time.
