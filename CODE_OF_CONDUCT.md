# Code of conduct

Lakeline is a small project run by one maintainer. This page says how people are expected to treat
each other here, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## What I expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. "This breaks when X" beats "this is bad".
- Respect that this is a side project: reviews and releases take time.
- Don't post other people's private information, and never paste real access tokens, workspace
  URLs you don't own, or customer data.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination or sexualized content.
- Spam, advertising, or pushing unrelated products.
- Trying to pass a fork or modified build off as the official Lakeline (see
  [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)).
- Posting security vulnerabilities publicly. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions, and block
accounts that break these rules. Decisions are the maintainer's alone.

## Reporting a problem

To report conduct that breaks these rules, open a private report at
https://github.com/vivkv07/lakeline-for-databricks/security/advisories/new and say it is a conduct
report. Only the maintainer can see it. Reports are kept private. I won't take action against someone for reporting in good faith.

This page is plain-language and original; it isn't a copy of another project's code of conduct.
